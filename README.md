# JAS Gam

_Just Another Simple_ Game!

"what an unoriginal name" you may say - YES ik! 

## What is this?

As the title suggests it is just another simple godot game. My plans were to do something related to the discontinued BBC TV show [NumberJacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numberjacks) hence the graphics in `/assest` but gave up and just made a dumbed down version of my main aim.

## Why you make this?

I'm hoping to org [Haven](https://haven.hackclub.com) London, one of over 200 game jams happening all over the world this November by [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com)! While I'm not a confirmed event orginiser yet, I've still made this game ofor [Haven Jumpstart](https://haven.jumpstart.hackclub.com/)!

## How do I play?

Just head to my [itch.io](https://hippogriff101.itch.io/) profile, [hippogriff101](https://hippogriff101.itch.io/) and click on JAS!

Or, if you hate yourself, clone the repo and import it into the latest version of Godot!

### Controls

- `W A S D` // `SPACE` // `Arrow Keys` for movement

- hug the ladder sprite to ride it

you might have to jump into the abyss and hope for the best - not the most intuitive ig, womp womp

if you get it faster than 20 seconds dm me on hc slack with a screenshot for a dog photo *
